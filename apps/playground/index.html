    Questions

    Before you open an account

    How long does a credit application take?

    Most are approved within one business day. Until then you can buy at the counter with a card.

    Can my crew order on my account?

    Yes. Add each buyer with their own sign-in and a spending limit. Orders over the limit wait for your approval.

    Do you cut threaded rod to length?

    At Akron and Canton, while you wait, in diameters up to 1". Toledo cuts orders overnight.

    Three branches, one stock count

    Halvorsen started as a single bolt counter on Home Avenue in 1962. We still run the counters the same way: the person at the counter knows the part, and the count on the screen is the count on the shelf.

    What “in stock” means here

    • Counts refresh every fifteen minutes from the warehouse scanners.
    • A part shows as in stock only when it’s on a shelf you can pick from today.
    • Transfers between branches run overnight, so “by transfer” means tomorrow morning.

    We would rather tell you a part is out than have you drive over for it.
