Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Write to us.

Put the question people ask most first

Answer in a sentence or two, then link to the full docs page if there is more to say.

How long does it take to set up?

Give a real time and list what the reader needs before they start.

Can I cancel at any time?

Say what happens to the reader’s data and to any payment when they leave.

Do you offer discounts?

List who qualifies, such as students or nonprofits, and how to ask.
